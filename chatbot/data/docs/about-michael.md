# About Michael Swayney

Michael Troy Swayney is an Army veteran transitioning into AI use case
development, with a focus on practical, real-world problem-solving. He is based
in Charleston, South Carolina.

He has been working with multiple large language models (LLMs) for about three
years. For the last six months his focus has been designing an AI-powered,
all-in-one restaurant app that handles everything from scheduling and payroll to
front-of-house → back-of-house continuity, seating, and HR.

## Background

Michael first got interested in tech in the early 2000s — editing the PC game
Driver and his MySpace profile while learning HTML and CSS from his uncle.

After serving in the Army (he left the service in 2007), he spent years in food
and beverage, moved into the car wash industry in 2015, returned to restaurants
in 2023, and most recently worked in data center construction from 2024 to 2026.

Working in restaurants for so long taught him where products like Toast POS fall
short — and closing gaps like that is exactly what he wants to do with AI.

## How he works

Michael keeps a growing portfolio of projects. He enjoys rapid prototyping,
iterating, and turning messy operational problems into practical AI solutions.
He brings hands-on operational experience, a product mindset, and the discipline
of his military service.

He's happy to talk about product pain points, AI prototypes, or trade tech
throwback stories.
